A passkey lets you sign in with your fingerprint, your face or your device's screen lock. It is stored by your device or password manager, it cannot be guessed, and it works only on this server. A passkey also counts as your [two-step verification](help://core:security-mfa).

## Add a passkey

1. Open **Settings → Security**.
2. In **Passkeys**, select **Add a passkey**.
3. Confirm with your fingerprint, face or screen lock when your device asks.

In the mobile app, a browser sheet opens on this server's page for this step. Confirm there, and the app continues.

To change a passkey's name, select the pencil next to it.

## Sign in with a passkey

On the sign-in screen, select **Sign in with a passkey** and confirm on your device. You do not need to type your username or password.

If you sign in with your password and two-step verification is on, you can also select **Use a passkey** instead of typing a code.

If an administrator gave you a temporary password, signing in with a passkey first asks you to choose a new password. In the mobile app this happens in the browser sheet; after you save it, the app asks you to sign in again.

## Remove a passkey

In **Settings → Security**, select the bin next to the passkey, then **Remove**. Also remove it from your device's password manager.
